About the Author – Jan Hamel Solomon

As an international educator, Christian relief worker, and owner of Key Sailing Charters, Jan Hamel Solomon defines “Women on the Move”. 40,000 guests agree, enjoying peace on earth from the “Key Breeze” with Jan and her childhood sweetheart, Tim. Author of “Sailing Home Sarasota”, Jan lives the book daily, and whether her guests are from South Africa, Siberia, or South America, they probably just followed this beach girl home.
